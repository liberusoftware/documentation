# Getting started with a Liberu application

Use this guide to find your work, make a change, and check its result. Liberu applications are configured by each organization, so names, available features and screen layouts can differ. Your administrator can tell you which application and features your organization uses.

## Find the right place

1. Open the application link supplied by your organization and sign in using its instructions. If you cannot sign in or need an account, contact your organization's support or administrator.
2. If you can choose an organization, team or workspace, check that the right one is selected before opening or changing records. You may see different information in each context.
3. Choose your product in the [project guide list](projects/README.md), then open its module guide for the area of work. A module is a group of related features, such as sales pipelines.
4. Search or browse for the record you need. Check its name and other identifying details before making a change. An empty list may mean there are no matching records, a filter is active, you are in another workspace, or you do not have access.

The project and module pages include planned experiences as well as source-reviewed information. A page does not guarantee that a feature is available in your installation. Look for its availability note and ask your administrator if an action is missing.

## Make and check a change

1. Open the relevant record and review its current information and status. Choose the action that matches your goal; review its consequences before confirming.
2. Complete the required fields and submit. If the application highlights errors, correct them and submit again. Keep a copy of lengthy text before navigating away if you are unsure whether the form saves drafts.
3. Read the result and return to the record or activity view to check the change. A message saying work is queued or processing means it is still in progress; check again for the final result.
4. If a request times out or the connection drops, check the record or operation status before repeating the action. This can help avoid duplicate submissions. If another person changed the record, reload and review the latest information before trying again.

For a concrete example, see [working with CRM sales pipelines](projects/crm/SALES-PIPELINES.md). The exact actions available depend on your application and permissions.

## When you need help

| What you see | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| An action or record is missing | Check the selected team and any filters. Ask an administrator whether the feature and your access are enabled. |
| A field is rejected | Read the message beside the field, correct the value and submit again. Ask for help if the requirement is unclear. |
| Access is denied | Confirm the workspace and ask an administrator for the access needed. Do not share your password. |
| Work is pending | Check the indicated status or activity page later. Contact support if it remains pending longer than your organization's expected processing time. |
| An error or uncertain result | Check the record before retrying. Give support the page, approximate time, action, and any displayed reference number; do not send passwords or sensitive record details unless your organization's support process requires them. |

Sign out when you finish on a shared device. Your organization's policies govern account access, data handling and support.

See [understanding status and recovering your work](UNDERSTANDING-STATUS.md) for more help with pending work, conflicts and interrupted submissions.
