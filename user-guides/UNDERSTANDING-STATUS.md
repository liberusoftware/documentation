# Understand status and recover your work

Use this guide when an application shows a status, rejects a change, or stops responding. Status names and available recovery options vary by product and organization; follow the message on your screen and your organization's procedures.

## Check what happened

After submitting a form or action, read the message and check the relevant record or activity page. A request can be accepted for processing before the underlying work is complete. Keep any reference number or link shown so you can find the operation again.

| What the screen says | What it means for your next step |
| --- | --- |
| Saved, completed or successful | Check the record or result to confirm it matches what you intended. |
| Queued, pending or processing | Work may still be in progress. Revisit the status or activity page if one is provided; wait for a final result before treating it as complete. |
| Failed | Read the message and check whether the record changed before retrying. Follow the recovery instructions or contact support. |
| Draft or unsynced | Your work may not yet be available to other people. Check the application's save or sync instructions before closing the page or device. |
| Access denied | Confirm the selected team or workspace, then ask an administrator about the access needed. |
| Conflict or changed by someone else | Reload or compare the latest version if the application offers that option. Review the current information before making the change again. |

The exact meaning of a status belongs to the product and task. For example, an operation can finish processing while its business outcome still needs review. Check the task guide for the result you expect.

## Recover after an interruption

1. If the page is still open, preserve any text you can safely copy in accordance with your organization's data policies. Do not assume a draft has been saved.
2. Restore your connection or sign in again if necessary. Check that you are in the same team or workspace.
3. Open the record or operation and look for the result of your earlier action. Check its status and relevant details before submitting again; a timeout alone does not show whether the request succeeded.
4. If there is no clear result, follow the task's recovery instructions or contact support. Avoid repeating actions that could create a duplicate, send a message, or affect a payment until the outcome is clear.

## Ask for help

Tell your organization's support team which application and page you used, what you tried to do, when the problem occurred, the message you saw, and any reference number. Include the selected team if relevant. Follow your organization's process for sharing screenshots or record details, and never send your password or authentication code.
