# Service Catalog Composition

**Package:** `module-liberu-service-catalog-composition`

Owns Liberu-specific presentation of software, hosting, consulting, support, and managed services: comparison groups, eligibility hints, plan-to-journey mapping, brand placement, and handoff metadata. Billing/Ecommerce own catalog, pricing, checkout, orders, subscriptions, payments, tax, and refunds.

**Public contract:** service family, source product reference, audience/brand visibility, capability summary, comparison attributes, freshness, and the authoritative checkout/lead URL. Never calculate a final price or promise provisioning locally.

**Acceptance:** accurate source freshness, brand/site visibility, accessible comparison on mobile, consent-aware enquiry, checkout/CRM handoff, unavailable-product fallback, and no divergence from Billing/Ecommerce authority.
