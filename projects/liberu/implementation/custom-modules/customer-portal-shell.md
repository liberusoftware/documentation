# Customer Portal Shell

**Package:** `module-liberu-customer-portal-shell`

Owns authenticated portal navigation, capability discovery, safe deep links, status composition, support launcher, and shared customer layout across Hosting, Services, and Software. CRM, Billing, Control Panel, Ecommerce, and Projects retain the records and decisions shown inside the shell.

**Public contract:** authorized capability cards, source resource links, freshness, status severity, next action, and support route. Hide unavailable capabilities without revealing entitlement or resource existence; never bypass source-module policies.

**Acceptance:** customer/staff separation, tenant and brand isolation, accessible responsive layout, direct links, expired sessions, partial provider outage, queued operations, consent, and portal/mobile parity.
